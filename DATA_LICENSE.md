# Data Licensing Policy

The Astro ABM software license does not automatically apply to data consumed or
generated by the project.

## Not Redistributed

The repository intentionally excludes real local market-data files, research
snapshots, QuestDB volumes, Parquet exports, generated Worldline reports, and
LLM presets. Examples and schemas are provided only to document expected input
formats.

In particular, the repository does not redistribute the local SPX, Gold, DXY,
or credit-proxy CSV files referenced by
`astro_research/data/local/LOCAL_DATA_PROVENANCE.json`.

## User Responsibility

Users who fetch or supply data are responsible for:

- accepting the source provider's current terms;
- using their own API credentials where required;
- respecting attribution, retention, publication, and redistribution limits;
- confirming whether commercial use is permitted;
- documenting transformations such as forward filling or proxy construction.

The built-in fetch and bootstrap commands are convenience tools. They do not
grant a license to the resulting data.

## Generated Outputs

Research reports and Worldline outputs may contain summaries derived from
third-party inputs. Before publishing or commercializing those outputs, review
the underlying source terms and ensure that no restricted raw data is exposed.

## Provenance

Data-source metadata, coverage, transformations, and caveats should remain
recorded in source registries and provenance manifests. Provenance metadata is
not a substitute for permission to redistribute the referenced data.
